The claim on incident #4471 that one fix covers the ticket is not supported by the rendered evidence. Split its parser and hint problems into separate tickets. A known status records awareness, not resolution.

The hint problem occurs through booking-v3 forms for day-first tenants. The field prints MM/DD/YYYY while the form interprets the entry using the tenant’s day-first convention. Align the displayed hint with the form’s actual parser. For example, R-41230 reports 6 January appearing as 1 June for copper.studio. How the desk should triage now

  1. Deduplicate export rows by report ID, then select the ticket being investigated.
  2. Read the body and look up the tenant’s locale and booking-form version.
  3. Match a supported cause using that configuration and the report’s channel.
  4. Merge only when the cause matches. A shared subject is insufficient; different subjects can still describe the same cause.
  5. Keep unknown tenants and contradictory symptoms for investigation. Do not force them into a known cause merely because their subject looks familiar.
  6. Track incoming and merged timestamps separately; review continuing reports and regression evidence before closing either ticket.
